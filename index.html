Implementation notes

This app follows the text you provided. For straight points, it scores every valid 2-card, 3-card, and 4-card rank subset with no more than a 3-card internal gap, then adds the applicable Ace/wheel bonuses. Duplicate ranks are collapsed for straight evaluation. Trips/quads receive no pair points, matching “award no points to any hand that contains three of the same rank.” The QQ99 example in the original text appears to say “pair of eights,” but it means nines.
